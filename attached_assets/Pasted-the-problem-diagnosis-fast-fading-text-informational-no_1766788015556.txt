the problem (diagnosis, fast)
	•	fading text = informational, not emotional
	•	multiple headlines = indecision instead of authority
	•	same subline repeating = cognitive noise
	•	motion exists, but it’s decorative, not meaningful

you’re saying “systems that don’t require explanation” while… explaining. the hero should demonstrate clarity, not describe it.

⸻

the shift: from “cycler” → “reveal”

stop rotating ideas.
reveal one truth at a time.

think: apple product launch pacing, not marketing site animation.

⸻

hero concept: “arrival, not rotation”

structure (one idea, three beats)

instead of cycling phrases, you arrive at the final line.

beat 1 — emergence
	•	text appears incomplete, slightly obscured
	•	low contrast, subtle blur, or masked
	•	feels like it’s coming into focus

example (barely visible at first):

operational

beat 2 — clarification
	•	second word resolves with precision
	•	spacing tightens
	•	opacity locks in

operational clarity

beat 3 — conviction
	•	period appears last (important)
	•	micro-scale snap or weight change
	•	everything stops moving

operational clarity.

then… stillness.
no more animation. no cycling. confidence lives in silence.

⸻

motion principles (this is key)

nothing should loop.
looping implies uncertainty.

use:
	•	one-time entrance
	•	easing: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)
	•	duration: 800–1200ms total, broken into micro-events
	•	delay between beats: 120–180ms (felt, not seen)

⸻

typography behavior (felt, not flashy)
	•	letter-spacing subtly animates from +1% → normal
	•	font-weight shifts slightly (e.g. 450 → 500)
	•	no vertical slide — slides feel like content blocks
	•	opacity from 0.6 → 1.0 (never 0 → 1)

this mimics focus, not motion.

⸻

background: supportive, not starry

the starfield is fine if it behaves like atmosphere, not content.

rules:
	•	ultra-slow drift (minutes, not seconds)
	•	no parallax tied to scroll
	•	grain > sparkle
	•	opacity capped low so text dominates

the hero text should feel like it’s cut into space, not floating on it.

⸻

subline treatment (if you keep one)

don’t explain. underline the feeling.

systems that don’t require explanation

rendered at:
	•	60–65% opacity

⸻

what to remove (important)
	•	rotating slogans
	•	repeating subcopy under every headline
	•	“strategy meets craft / built for impact” in hero
