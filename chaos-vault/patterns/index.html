Operating Position
Preserve agency and authorship

The library does not treat a face, cursor, voice, avatar choice, sticker, streak, or model confidence score as privileged access to a person’s internal state. It also does not let a generator quietly expand from proposing content into controlling routing, persistence, publication, or external actions. Adaptation and automation remain visible, bounded, reversible, and user-owned.

Import Standard
Original inventionPreserve product provenance and authorship. Extract the portable contract without pretending every implementation is production-ready.
Research-linked hypothesisKeep evidence labels, failure modes, competing explanations, and falsification conditions attached.
Clean-room reconstructionRebuild behavior from audited contracts rather than copying domain-specific source or demo fiction.
Replit-origin repositorySearch commit history, inspect expected product paths on the active ref, and fetch representative files directly before trusting metadata or launch stubs.
Generated artifact systemSeparate design memory, generation request, execution authority, rendering, persistence, and publication. Never let one opaque payload control all five.
Applied specimenAn implementation illustrates a contract. It does not validate general effectiveness, causality, safety, or clinical value.
Quarantined materialDocument why it must not be imported. Do not quietly rehabilitate fake scores, fake quotes, fake hardware, open proxies, arbitrary generated execution, punitive economies, manipulative streak systems, or automatic status sharing.
