Operating Position
State hypotheses, not emotional facts

The library does not treat a face, cursor, voice, streak, or model confidence score as privileged access to a person’s internal state. Adaptation begins with universal support or an explicit user request, remains visible and reversible, expires at a bounded scope, and preserves a route back to the complete interface.

Import Standard
Original inventionPreserve product provenance and authorship. Extract the portable contract without pretending every implementation is production-ready.
Research-linked hypothesisKeep evidence labels, failure modes, competing explanations, and falsification conditions attached.
Clean-room reconstructionRebuild behavior from audited contracts rather than copying domain-specific source or demo fiction.
Quarantined materialDocument why it must not be imported. Do not quietly rehabilitate fake scores, fake quotes, fake hardware, or manipulative streak systems.
