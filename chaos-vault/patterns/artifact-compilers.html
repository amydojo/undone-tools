Artifact Compilers

Systems that transform invisible cost, interpretation, memory, or evidence into a bounded digital object whose form carries meaning.

Cross-product languageConstrained generation
Category Thesis
Affective Utility Objects

Small digital machines that perform one psychologically meaningful job while preserving dignity, continuity, and agency. They are not generic dashboards, chatbots, or decorative skeuomorphism. The object establishes a bounded ritual, a stable output, and a clear ending.

CV-ART-001

Interpretation-Conditioned Design

Meaning changes the form of the output, not only its copy. Glint’s strongest primitive mapped scene interpretation into a bounded template, layout, palette, gradient, and hierarchy.

InputStructured interpretation with source provenance and explicit uncertainty.
CompilerInspectible mapping into a finite design system rather than arbitrary generated markup.
QuarantineEmotion stereotypes, fake calibrated confidence, or generated dialogue presented as an authentic quote.
CV-ART-002

Emotional Artifact Compiler

Convert an invisible event into a stable object: a day becomes a receipt, a scene becomes a card, an ordinary possession becomes an archival specimen. The artifact gives the interpretation structure without claiming it proves private truth.

StagesCapture → structure → validate → render → preserve or release.
RendererFixed app-owned components, templates, filenames, and export behavior.
BoundaryThe model may propose content. It does not control layout, persistence, actions, or executable markup.
CV-ART-003

Bounded Machine Metaphor

Temporarily turn the phone into one physical-feeling appliance with one emotional job. Bad Day Receipt becomes a printer. Capsule becomes a preservation chamber. Face Value becomes a skincare fridge that holds observations under stable conditions.

Must haveOne job, a material ritual, a clear start, a clear finish, and an ordinary accessibility explanation.
May removeNonessential app chrome and unrelated navigation during the bounded ritual.
Failure modeA charming metaphor that adds steps, hides state, or exists only for a Dribbble screenshot.
CV-ART-004

Provenance-Preserving Artifact

Capsule separates the source capture, the user-locked specimen, and generated metadata. The source remains available as provenance while the artifact becomes the primary presentation.

ObservedSource image, captured context, timestamp, user-entered object history.
DerivedSpecimen crop, observations, labels, visual treatment, generated metadata.
DiscloseWhat was observed, manually selected, inferred, generated, or supplied by fallback logic.
CV-ART-005

Format Carries Meaning

The container changes the psychological reading of the content. A receipt itemizes invisible cost. A specimen card creates attention and preservation. An archive seal establishes continuity. A fridge drawer implies controlled comparison over time.

Choose byThe cognitive and emotional job the format performs, not aesthetic novelty.
Keep legibleThe literal action, state, and consequence beneath the metaphor.
System valueRecurring artifact grammars can become a recognizable Lab Dojo product language.

source + user context

  → structured artifact record
  → validate provenance and forbidden claims
  → select from app-owned templates
  → render stable artifact
  → archive, export, release, or carry forward
