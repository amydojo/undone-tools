HARVEST 03 · CATMODE + GET FRIDGE + STICKEROS

Affective Artifact Infrastructure

Convert a messy condition into a bounded artifact, reduce the next interaction to an operational mode, keep repair nearby, and preserve only the evidence needed for future re-entry.

14 Harvested Patterns3 Verified DonorsIllustrates, Does Not Validate
Recovered Product Grammar
Situation → artifact → operating mode → repair → record

These prototypes do not share a market or visual style. They share a deeper architecture. A room becomes a prescription, ingredients become a cooking appliance, and a changing status becomes a sendable object. The durable invention is the transformation contract, not the donor-specific demo.

Donor 01 · Catmode · Verified 1bb108f

Environmental behavior becomes a testable prescription.

CV-AFI-001

Behavioral Loop Prescription

Model a recurring behavior as a sequence of needs, then design a route that lets the sequence complete without repeatedly falling into the harmful default path.

Control
Editable assumptions, low-cost trial, observation period, and reversal.
Risk
Designer confidence impersonating behavioral causality.
CV-AFI-002

Route Before Object

Find the missing connection between useful elements before recommending another object, purchase, feature, or screen.

Control
Existing-condition map, connection hypothesis, and a no-purchase path.
Risk
Minimalism used to deny a genuinely necessary resource.
CV-AFI-003

Subject-Decides-Outcome

The being most affected by an intervention determines whether it works through observable use, refusal, friction, or recovery.

Control
Baseline, refusal, observable outcomes, and competing explanations.
Risk
Selective observation or anthropomorphic interpretation.
CV-AFI-004

Medium-Translated Intervention

Express one intervention through media designed for different jobs: screen for understanding, document for preservation, and field sheet for observation.

Control
Shared source of truth, format-specific hierarchy, and stable identity.
Risk
Duplicate formats drifting into contradictory records.
CV-AFI-005

Use-What-You-Have Trial

Test intervention logic with reversible existing materials before turning uncertainty into a purchase recommendation.

Control
Time limit, stop condition, reversibility, and permission to fail.
Risk
Romanticizing scarcity or delaying necessary equipment.
Donor 02 · Get Fridge · Verified 919becd

A general-purpose phone becomes one calm task appliance.

CV-AFI-006

Appliance Mode

Temporarily turn a general-purpose screen into one task appliance with one visible step, stable controls, nearby recovery, and no unrelated feed pressure.

Control
Exit, previous, next, complete plan, and stable layout.
Risk
Mode trapping or hidden consequential context.
CV-AFI-007

Available-Materials Compiler

Compile a useful next action from the materials already present instead of beginning with an idealized acquisition plan.

Control
Editable inventory, missing-item disclosure, regenerate, and safe boundaries.
Risk
Unsafe substitutions or hallucinated availability.
CV-AFI-008

Choice Surrender Control

Let the person explicitly delegate one bounded decision when choosing is the burden, while keeping the result inspectable and reversible.

Control
Bounded candidates, try another, manual choice, and no auto-commit.
Risk
Randomness used for consequential decisions.
CV-AFI-009

Quiet Satisficing Completion

Acknowledge that the necessary thing happened without demanding celebration, optimization, momentum, or a positive emotional performance.

Control
Neutral language, skip feedback, no forced sharing, and a clean exit.
Risk
Patronizing reassurance or concealed poor outcomes.
CV-AFI-010

Evidence-by-Use Archive

Let completed use, not generated possibility, determine what earns a durable place in the personal archive.

Control
Explicit save, delete, notes, provenance, and no silent profile.
Risk
One use mistaken for a permanent preference.
Existing records strengthened

Get Fridge supplies direct implementation evidence for Something Feels Off? Recovery Reveal and Bounded Machine Metaphor. Those records are strengthened rather than duplicated.

Donor 03 · StickerOS · Verified 4c23eaf

Ambient state becomes a user-owned object inside ordinary communication.

CV-AFI-011

Ambient State Object

Represent a small changing state as a sendable object rather than requiring a dashboard or explanatory paragraph.

Control
User choice, preview, freshness, text equivalent, and expiration.
Risk
Stale state presented as live or coerced availability signaling.
CV-AFI-012

Context Without Confession

Communicate operational context through a chosen artifact without requiring emotional explanation, diagnosis, or intimate disclosure.

Control
Neutral option, custom wording, privacy, and no recipient entitlement.
Risk
The artifact becoming surveillance or a social obligation.
CV-AFI-013

Cross-Surface Expressive Continuity

Keep one user-owned expressive object coherent across library, editor, keyboard, message surface, and share extension.

Control
Least privilege, consistent deletion, versioned model, and fallbacks.
Risk
Permission confusion, partial deletion, or surface divergence.
CV-AFI-014

Local Context Suggestion

Rank user-authored expressive objects against small local signals without uploading conversation content, generating a message, or claiming to know the person.

Control
Inspectable factors, disable, manual search, no auto-send, and minimization.
Risk
Device telemetry treated as affect or suggestion presented as prediction.
Quarantine
DO NOT IMPORT LITERALLY
CLAIMS Prototype implementation illustrates a pattern. It does not validate general effectiveness, causality, or clinical benefit.
CARE Do not delay safety equipment, veterinary care, food-safety guidance, allergy review, or other necessary resources.
PRIVACY Never auto-send energy, availability, battery, activity, location, or other contextual state.
INFERENCE A selected status object is not evidence of mood, diagnosis, personality, or relationship intent.
ACCESS Full-access keyboard permissions and network services must not become hidden prerequisites for basic expression.
