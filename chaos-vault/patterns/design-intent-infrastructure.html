Design Intent Infrastructure

Portable contracts for preserving authorship while the system absorbs compatibility and production burden. The original arbitrary endpoint relay was removed rather than rehabilitated.

Recovered System Thesis
Give the generator structured memory

Do not ask a generator to remember the design through prompt atmosphere. Encode the design system, keep the generation request inspectable, constrain execution to approved shells, and preserve final authority with the person.

Five Portable Patterns
CV-DIN-001

Design Intent Serialization

Visual, semantic, component, motion, responsive, and compatibility decisions become structured machine memory with version and provenance.

CV-DIN-002

Human-Inspectable Generation Contract

Intent and generation instructions remain separate, readable, editable, and reviewable before execution. Collisions are reported rather than silently overwritten.

CV-DIN-003

Reference-Constrained Artifact Compiler

Variable content enters bounded slots inside an approved compatibility shell. Hostile-runtime infrastructure is tested and retained rather than regenerated casually.

CV-DIN-004

Mechanical Burden Transfer

The system absorbs repetitive production labor without taking taste, meaning, authorship, or approval away from the person.

CV-DIN-005

Compatibility as Care

The intended experience includes the environments where people actually receive it. Reliable fallback and legibility are affective obligations, not cleanup work.
