Capytopia Patterns

A playable café world containing a stepwise drink ritual, expressive capybara customization, sound and motion feedback, quests, game state, and spatial zones. The GitHub connector incorrectly reported the repository as empty; direct commit and file inspection proved otherwise.

Why It Matters
Play as affective infrastructure

Capytopia does not need to read or diagnose a person to change the emotional quality of an interaction. It creates a small, legible world where actions are concrete, sensory, low-stakes, and visibly accumulative. The strongest portable lesson is not “make everything cute.” It is to give abstract progress a body, place, rhythm, and ending.

CV-CAP-001 · RECONSTRUCTED

Sensory Assembly Ritual

A making flow becomes a three-stage ritual. Each ingredient choice advances a visible sequence, optionally produces a corresponding sound, and accumulates as a new layer inside the cup preview. The object remembers the user’s actions so progress is seen rather than merely announced.

Portable valueGround abstract progress in visible material change. Useful for onboarding, creative tools, care tasks, guided setup, and any process where “what I have done” should remain perceptible.
Observed mechanicsBase → milk → topping; pour, steam, and stir audio; animated cup layers; progress strip; explicit completion.
Required safeguardsMute, reduced motion, textual state, cancel, and no essential meaning carried by sound.
Do not claimThat sensory feedback is inherently calming, regulating, or therapeutic.
CV-CAP-002 · RECONSTRUCTED

Avatar-Mediated Self-Expression

The user can choose a character expression, color, blush state, held object, and clothing while seeing the result immediately. This creates expressive distance: a person can make the character sleepy, neutral, excited, or dressed for a mood without having to declare that the label describes them.

Portable valueSupport symbolic expression without personal interrogation. Particularly useful where direct emotional labels would feel invasive or over-serious.
AuthorityThe user selects every expressive state. The interface must not infer or diagnose from avatar choices.
Required safeguardsNeutral option, reset, immediate preview, privacy by default, and no identity-expression paywall pressure.
Failure modeTreating a playful choice as psychological telemetry.
CV-CAP-003 · EXPERIMENTAL

Soft-World Task Scaffolding

The café divides responsibilities into recognizable places: a service counter, kitchen, seating area, and garden patio. Each zone carries a name, visual treatment, icon, and plain-language purpose. The environment begins to function as a spatial task map rather than a decorative background.

Portable valueUse place to reduce abstract navigation. A bounded world can make categories and next actions easier to remember than nested menus.
Observed structureNamed zones, capybara entities, quests, coins, levels, current tasks, and café layout state.
Current limitationZone hit detection and actions remain unfinished in the verified snapshot. This is a promising scaffold, not a completed navigation system.
Required safeguardsList equivalent, keyboard and touch access, visible active zone, and no essential action hidden only inside a canvas.
