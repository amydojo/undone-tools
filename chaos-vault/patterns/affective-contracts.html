Affective Contracts

Contracts for interfaces that adapt to workload, uncertainty, interruption, or deviation without pretending to know the person’s private emotional truth.

5 Research-linked1 Product-native
Invariant
Condition, not character

Respond to a bounded interaction condition such as task complexity, retries, interruption, or a user request. Do not turn temporary difficulty into a personality, diagnosis, or permanent profile.

CV-AFX-001 · EVIDENCE H

Load-Adaptive Disclosure

Temporarily reduce simultaneous secondary information when demand is high while keeping the complete view one action away. The system changes presentation, not the user’s rights or the task’s actual consequences.

Use whenThe task has one clear primary action and secondary information is noncritical.
Must preserveShow everything, stable state, undo, and no silent information removal.
Do notHide legal, medical, financial, safety, or comparative information.
CV-AFX-002 · EVIDENCE H

Gentle Reentry

After interruption, avoidance, failure, or absence, restore the last meaningful context, identify what changed, and offer one neutral next action. A return should not be treated as a retention opportunity or a moral report card.

AnatomyRecognize return, restore context, separate completed and unresolved work, offer one low-commitment action.
ControlsContinue, review changes, restart, defer, or discard.
BanStreak loss, “you disappeared,” backlog intimidation, or urgency disguised as warmth.
CV-AFX-003 · EVIDENCE H

User-Correctable State

If a state hypothesis changes information density, pacing, notifications, or recommendations, the user can inspect what triggered it, correct it, suppress it, and see when it expires.

ShowTentative construct, evidence summary, scope, current effect, expiration.
CorrectionStop or update the adaptation and prevent immediate reinference from the same evidence.
NeverInterpret correction as training consent or create a permanent psychological profile.
CV-AFX-004 · EVIDENCE H

Calibrated Language

Wording should reflect the maturity and confidence of the evidence. The system distinguishes observation, interpretation, and suggestion while keeping competing explanations visible.

Prefer“This may indicate,” “one possible reading,” and “still taking shape.”
IncludeWhat was observed, what is inferred, alternatives, and correction.
Avoid“We detected your frustration with 87% confidence.” Model output is not private emotional truth.
CV-AFX-005 · EVIDENCE H

Reversible Simplification

Reduce interaction demand without silently removing capability. The adapted view is temporary, visible, stable, and at least as easy to leave as it was to enter.

ChangesSequence, disclosure, emphasis, optional work, and decorative motion.
Does not changeConsequences, access, required facts, commitments, or user authority.
Failure modeA calmer-looking interface that is less complete or harder to compare.
CV-AFX-006 · PRODUCT-NATIVE

“Something Feels Off?” Recovery Reveal

A quiet optional doorway embedded inside the task. It normalizes deviation, preserves agency, and offers specific repair without making failure the center of the experience. This originated in Fridge Web’s step-by-step cook mode, where recovery guidance appeared when a meal was going wrong.

TriggerLow-salience but accessible, available during the exact step where recovery is useful.
RevealSpecific symptoms, likely causes, safe adjustments, and a route back to the task.
Do not reduce it toA generic AI confidence accordion. Its primary invention is humane mid-task repair.
