CORE TOOL

Clinical Protocol

  • Keep upright for 4 hours post-treatment
  • Avoid touching or massaging injection sites
  • No intense exercise for 24 hours
  • Clean skin gently; avoid active topicals
  • Avoid extreme heat (saunas, hot tubs)
  • Minimize alcohol and blood-thinning supplements
  • Gentle skincare only
  • Monitor for any delayed swelling
  • Minor bruising and localized swelling
  • Mild tenderness at injection sites
  • Slight asymmetry (resolves as swelling subsides)
  • Temporary "tightness" in treated area
  • Skin blanching (pale/white spots)
  • Intense pain or "mottled" skin appearance
  • Vision changes or severe headache
  • Spreading redness or heat at site
