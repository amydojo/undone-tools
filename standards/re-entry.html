CLINICAL BOUNDARY

The provider authors the rules.

RE:ENTRY displays the issued protocol. It does not diagnose reactions, interpret symptoms, invent timing, or replace direct clinical guidance.

Pain, worsening symptoms, unexpected recovery, or anything absent from the issued protocol returns to the provider.
