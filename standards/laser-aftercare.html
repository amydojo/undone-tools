CORE TOOL

Clinical Protocol

  • Keep treated area cool and clean
  • Avoid hot showers or exercise for 24 hours
  • Use recommended soothing gels only
  • Wear loose, breathable clothing over treated area
  • Strict sun protection (SPF 30+ minimum)
  • Do not scrub or exfoliate treated area
  • Avoid active topicals (Retinol, AHA, BHA)
  • Let hair shed naturally; do not pluck
  • Redness and mild swelling (erythema)
  • Follicular edema (small bumps around follicles)
  • Sensation similar to a mild sunburn
  • Hair shedding 1–3 weeks post-treatment
  • Blistering or skin crusting
  • Persistent intense pain beyond 48 hours
  • Severe pigment changes (darkening or lightening)
  • Signs of infection (pus, spreading redness)
